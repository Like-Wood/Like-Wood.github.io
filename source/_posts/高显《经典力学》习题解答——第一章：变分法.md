## 1. 变分法

**1.1** *给定 $f(t)$ 的泛函 $S[f] = -\int\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d} t e^{-V(f(t))}\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}$，其中 $V$ 是 $f$ 的任意函数。求 $S[f]$ 取极值时，$f(t)$ 的欧拉-拉格朗日方程。*

**解：** 对 $S[f]$ 取变分，得
$$
\begin{align*}
\delta S &= \int {\rm d} t e^{-V(f(t))}\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}\frac{\partial V}{\partial f}\delta f + e^{-V(f(t))}\frac{f'(t)}{\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}}\delta f'(t)\\
&= \int {\rm d} t \left[e^{-V(f(t))}\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}\frac{\partial V}{\partial f} - \frac{\rm d}{{\rm d} t}\left(e^{-V(f(t))}\frac{f'(t)}{\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}}\right)\right]\delta f(t),
\end{align*}
$$
其中，用到了 $e^{-V(f(t))}\frac{f'(t)}{\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}}\delta f'(t) \simeq - \frac{\rm d}{{\rm d} t}\left(e^{-V(f(t))}\frac{f'(t)}{\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}}\right)\delta f(t)$。这样就得到了欧拉-拉格朗日方程为
$$
\frac{\rm d}{{\rm d} t}\left(e^{-V(f(t))}\frac{f'(t)}{\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}}\right) - e^{-V(f(t))}\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}\frac{\partial V}{\partial f} = 0.
$$
可以进一步展开得到
$$
e^{-V(f(t))}\frac{f''(t)}{\left(1-(f'(t))^2\right)^{3/2}} - e^{-V(f(t))}\frac{f'(t)}{\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}}\frac{\partial V}{\partial f}f'(t) - e^{-V(f(t))}\sqrt{1-(f'(t))^2}\frac{\partial V}{\partial f} = 0.
$$
